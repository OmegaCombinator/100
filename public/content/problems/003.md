## Formalization

This is a complete Acorn proof that the rational numbers are enumerated by the
natural numbers. The proof builds an explicit diagonal enumeration of natural
pairs, combines it with a zig-zag enumeration of the integers, and maps the
resulting integer-positive-denominator pairs into canonical rationals.

The public Top 100 wrapper states that there is a natural-number enumeration of
the rationals, and that the rationals contain an embedded copy of the natural
numbers:

```acorn

    exists(enumeration: Nat -> Rat) {
        is_surjective_fn(enumeration)

        is_injective_fn(embedding)

}
```

## Interpretation

This matches the classical countability argument: enumerate integer numerators
and positive natural denominators along diagonals, reduce each fraction to a
rational number, and observe that every rational appears in the enumeration.
