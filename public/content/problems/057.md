## Formalization

This is a complete Acorn proof of Heron's formula in an equivalent squared form
for real coordinate-plane triangles. The public Top 100 wrapper states:

```acorn
theorem top100_057_herons_formula_squared(
    a: Point2[Real], b: Point2[Real], c: Point2[Real],
    side_a: Real, side_b: Real, side_c: Real
) {
    point2_real_length_witness_57(c.sub(b), side_a) and
    point2_real_length_witness_57(c.sub(a), side_b) and
    point2_real_length_witness_57(b.sub(a), side_c)
    implies
    heron_linear_product(side_a, side_b, side_c) =
        (a.triangle_area2(b, c) + a.triangle_area2(b, c)) *
        (a.triangle_area2(b, c) + a.triangle_area2(b, c))
}
```

Here `side_a`, `side_b`, and `side_c` are nonnegative length witnesses for the
three side vectors. The function `heron_linear_product(a, b, c)` is the product
`(a + b + c)(-a + b + c)(a - b + c)(a + b - c)`. The quantity
`triangle_area2` is the signed doubled coordinate area, so the right side is the
square of four times the usual unsigned triangle area.

The proof adds reusable `geometry.point2_heron` infrastructure. It proves the
two-dimensional Lagrange identity for dot and cross products, expands Heron's
four linear factors as a difference of squares, and combines these with the side
length witness equations.

## Interpretation

This is equivalent to the classical statement `Area^2 = s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)`, but
avoids introducing square roots, absolute values, or division by two. Multiplying
the classical identity by `16` gives exactly the proved squared doubled-area
form.
